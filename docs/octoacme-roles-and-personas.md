# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers are responsible for user experience research, design, prototyping, and usability testing. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to ensure features meet usability criteria and drive customer satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing to inform design decisions
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity design artifacts
- Define and document UX acceptance criteria for features
- Collaborate with Developers to ensure accurate implementation of designs
- Gather and synthesize user feedback during sprint reviews

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce friction and support rate by validating designs before development
- Ensure features align with user needs and business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Sprint reviews and design critique sessions
- Design handoff notes and annotated prototypes
- Usability test reports and UX recommendations shared with PM and Developers

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers maintain CI/CD pipelines, manage release environments, and monitor deployment health. They interact with Developers and QA to ensure reliable, repeatable delivery and security.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation tooling
- Manage staging, pre-production, and production environments
- Monitor system health, performance, and deployment success
- Support incident response and coordinate rollback procedures
- Implement and enforce security controls in the delivery pipeline

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable software releases
- Minimize deployment failures and reduce mean time to recovery
- Improve observability and developer productivity

### Typical Communication
- Deployment status updates and release notes
- Incident postmortems and remediation plans
- Infrastructure change reviews with Developers and Security Lead

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define metrics, uncover actionable insights, and create dashboards for the team and stakeholders. They work with Product Managers and Project Managers to frame hypotheses and create data-driven decision mechanisms.

### Responsibilities
- Define, track, and report on key product and delivery metrics
- Build and maintain dashboards for team and stakeholder visibility
- Analyze data to surface trends, anomalies, and opportunities
- Support A/B testing and experiment design with Product Managers
- Measure and report on the impact of releases

### Goals
- Enable data-driven prioritization and decision-making
- Improve team visibility into outcomes and leading indicators
- Reduce time to insight for stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Regular reporting cadences with PMs and stakeholders
- Dashboard walkthroughs in sprint reviews and planning sessions
- Ad hoc analysis requests documented and shared via project channels

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads own security risk assessment, vulnerability reviews, and incident response plans. They partner with Developers and Project Managers for secure development and escalate critical issues per the risk communication protocol.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews and threat modeling for features and architecture changes
- Maintain vulnerability tracking and remediation plans
- Define and enforce secure development standards and guidelines
- Own incident response planning and coordinate security escalations
- Ensure compliance requirements are met across the delivery lifecycle

### Goals
- Minimize security risk exposure across all products and services
- Embed security practices early in the development lifecycle
- Ensure timely identification and resolution of vulnerabilities

### Typical Communication
- Security review sign-offs during feature planning and release gates
- Risk escalations per the risk communication protocol in `octoacme-risks-and-communication.md`
- Security findings reports shared with Developers and Project Managers

---

## Support Specialist

### Role Summary
Support Specialists handle user queries, triage incidents, organize FAQs, and escalate bugs or feedback. They collaborate with Product Managers and Project Managers to surface customer impact and improve documentation.

### Responsibilities
- Respond to and resolve user-reported incidents and queries
- Triage and reproduce bugs, escalating confirmed defects to the development team
- Maintain and improve user-facing documentation, FAQs, and self-service resources
- Track recurring issues and identify patterns to inform product improvements
- Communicate customer impact and urgency to Project Managers and Product Managers

### Goals
- Reduce user-facing incidents and time to resolution
- Improve self-service documentation to deflect common queries
- Ensure customer feedback reaches the product team promptly

### Typical Communication
- Bug and incident reports submitted via the project board
- Weekly summaries of top issues and customer impact shared with PMs
- Collaboration with PdMs and Developers on documentation updates and release notes

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts document business requirements, support stakeholder interviews, and validate alignment between technical outcomes and business objectives. They work closely with Project Managers and Product Managers to translate needs into clear project tasks.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business and functional requirements
- Facilitate stakeholder interviews and workshops to capture needs
- Map business processes and identify gaps or improvement opportunities
- Ensure traceability between requirements and delivered features
- Validate that technical solutions meet business objectives before release

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and acceptance criteria
- Improve alignment between business stakeholders and delivery teams
- Enable faster, higher-confidence decision-making through clear documentation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documents and user story refinement sessions with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Stakeholder briefings and sign-off reviews
- Traceability updates and change request documentation on the project board
